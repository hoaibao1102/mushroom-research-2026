# Day 5 — 5 November 2026

Today we tested one practical change for crowded scenes and checked whether the V2 depth maps are ready for physical size measurement. We also got the WB-only Gradio demo running.

## Dense-scene counting

Khang compared NMS IoU 0.70 and 0.50 on eight selected dense test images. Counting MAE fell from 3.375 to 1.875 (44.4% lower); six images improved and two worsened. The checkpoint and other inference settings stayed fixed. The result is preliminary: the sample is small, visual confirmation in the selection file is pending, and the full 450-image test set has not been evaluated at the new setting.

## RGB-D measurement feasibility

Thịnh checked 30 images and 4,970 WB masks, plus the depth-file types across the 450-image test split. Most masks had valid depth, but the depth scale and camera intrinsics were not verified, RGB-depth alignment remains uncertain, and some masks had little or no valid depth. No physical area in mm² was calculated; the project continues to report px².

## Interactive demo

The WB-only Gradio app was launched in Colab and confirmed working by the user. It loads a random test image or accepts an RGB upload, runs the existing checkpoint, displays masks/counts and exports image and CSV results. No training is performed.
