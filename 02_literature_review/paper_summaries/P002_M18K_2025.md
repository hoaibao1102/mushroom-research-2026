# P002 — M18K (Zakeri et al., 2025)

**Source:** Zakeri et al., *Computers* 14(5), 199. DOI: [10.3390/computers14050199](https://doi.org/10.3390/computers14050199). Baseline paper PDF was supplied by the team.

## Method and datasets

The paper presents RGB-D mushroom imagery, 2D detection and instance segmentation annotations, 3D pose annotations and separate longitudinal growth-monitoring images. The detection/segmentation/pose subset in the paper comprises **2,000 images**. Reported evaluations use 70% train, 10% validation and 20% unseen test, rather than the observed V2 80/10/10 split.

## Relevance and open comparison questions

The paper includes 3D pose estimation already, so any 3D pipeline we build is not inherently a new task. The authors' four-channel RGB-D segmentation underperformed the RGB baseline; alternative depth fusion could be studied with a controlled protocol. Novelty and feasibility require further literature review and resource estimates.
